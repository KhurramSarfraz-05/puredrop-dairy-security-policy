# PureDrop Dairy — Password Policy

**Client:** PureDrop Dairy  
**Document:** Password Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft for client review

## 1. Purpose
This policy explains how PureDrop Dairy staff must create and protect passwords used for company accounts and work-related systems.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees and other authorised people who use a PureDrop Dairy account, including accounts used for email, delivery operations, and company devices or services.

## 3. Password rules

1. **Use strong passwords.** Create passwords that are at least 12 characters long. A memorable passphrase made from several unrelated words is recommended.
2. **Use a different password for every account.** Never reuse a work password on personal websites or on another work service.
3. **Keep passwords private.** Do not share passwords with colleagues, friends, or family, and do not send them through chat, email, or other unsecured messages.
4. **Use multi-factor authentication where available.** Enable an additional sign-in check, such as an authenticator app, on company accounts that support it.
5. **Store passwords safely.** Use an organisation-approved password manager if one is provided. Do not write passwords on notes attached to a computer or leave them where other people can see them.
6. **Change a password when it may be exposed.** If a password is lost, shared, or suspected of being compromised, change it promptly and inform the person responsible for IT or security.
7. **Do not save work passwords on shared devices.** Sign out after using a shared or public computer, and do not allow the browser to remember the password on that device.

## 4. Staff responsibilities
Every staff member is responsible for following these rules and reporting suspected password exposure or unusual account activity to their manager or the designated IT/security contact.

## 5. Policy review
The policy owner should review this document periodically and update it when PureDrop Dairy's systems or working practices change.
